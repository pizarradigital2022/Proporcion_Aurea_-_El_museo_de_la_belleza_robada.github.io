Operación 00Φ: El museo de la belleza robada

English version

Introduction for students

Confidential message from the International Mathematics Agency · Classification: Top Secret

Agents, we have a mathematical emergency. Auric Goldfinger, the millionaire obsessed with gold, has decided that collecting gold bars, luxury cars and golden mansions is no longer enough. Now he is after a much more powerful treasure: the golden ratio, Φ, a mathematical relationship that appears in certain geometric shapes and is connected to the Fibonacci sequence.

His plan is as ridiculous as it is dangerous: he wants to erase every reference to Φ from human memory. To do this, his henchmen are searching for works of art, buildings and elements of nature that may contain clues about this ratio. If he manages to destroy all the evidence, nobody will be able to study its properties or discover its mathematical connections. Goldfinger wants to take control of beauty itself!

But Agent Fibonacci, also known as 00Φ, has come up with a brilliant trap. Instead of hiding the evidence, he suggests bringing it together in a secret museum inside the school. Each team of agents will have to investigate a work of art or an element of nature, find reliable information, carry out calculations and check whether there really is a mathematical connection with the golden ratio or the Fibonacci sequence. It will not be enough to repeat what we find on the internet: we will need evidence, measurements and arguments that can stand up to any interrogation.

Your mission is to work as a team, discover the properties of Φ, recreate the Fibonacci spiral and prepare an exhibition that will lure Goldfinger into the trap. To do this, you will create a mathematical infographic and an audio guide explaining your discovery. You will also have to organise the tasks, take responsibility, listen to other people's ideas and assess your own contribution.

Remember: Goldfinger has money, henchmen and what seems to be a perfect plan. But you have something much more dangerous for his plans: mathematics, critical thinking and teamwork.

Agents, time is running out. Will you be able to solve the mystery before Goldfinger makes the last clue disappear?

General introduction

What do a numerical sequence, a spiral, a work of art and a sunflower have in common? Is it true that the golden ratio appears everywhere, or are some of these connections myths that we need to check?

Operation 00Φ: Goldfinger’s Last Secret is a Mathematics learning project for 4th ESO (in Spain) that turns these questions into an investigation mission. Through a spy story, students face the challenge of understanding the golden ratio and the Fibonacci sequence, exploring their properties and examining possible examples in art, architecture and nature.

The adventure ends with the creation of a small mathematical museum. Each team contributes an object they have researched, an explanatory infographic and an audio guide. The mission is not just to find surprising connections, but to use calculations, measurements and reliable sources to explain which claims are supported by evidence and which cannot be proven.

In this way, mathematics goes beyond the textbook and becomes a tool for exploration, communication and critical thinking. And, in the face of Goldfinger’s plan, students discover that knowledge cannot belong to just one person: it is built, checked and shared.
